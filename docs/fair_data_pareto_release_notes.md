## Evidence and limitations

- Catalog build: 427 assets, 718 normalized records; 383 assets have a source-register/local-metadata link, 279 contain a DOI-like identifier, 45 have no source-register link.
- Canonical parameter registry: 538 rows, zero quality-D rows; 529 are class E, seven project controls, and two unclassified. The normalized master has 690 E, 16 B, one A, seven controls and four unclassified records. This is not evidence that all inputs are literature-grade.
- The overall dataset has no minted DOI. Content hashes and repository URLs are useful identifiers but do not replace a persistent DOI. A public versioned repository deposit is required to mint one.
- Protein recovery literature is not fully transferable: candidate sources use total nitrogen and/or include fiber-bound nitrogen. They remain flagged as proxies; the default structural recovery value was not silently changed.
- RVO grant and operating-support amounts are hypothetical what-if scenarios, not confirmed eligibility or awards. Biomethane support uses a screening proxy. Current investment metrics are conditional screening estimates, not investment-grade TEA.
- Current Pareto batches show positive proxy NPV only for some supported scenarios. Under the sampled designs, no positive-NPV rows occur at €0 or €10/wet t grass bonus; positives appear at higher bonuses with some grant combinations. The best sampled proxy NPV is about €8.26m (protein enabled) and €7.53m (protein bypass). These are sampled results, not global optima or validated business cases.
- Pareto climate reporting remains a partial operational inventory; substitution-credit objectives and validated LCA are not complete. Costing warnings, partial CAPEX/OPEX, missing supply-chain burdens and assumptions remain visible.
- At zero CAPEX grant, the sampled positive-NPV counts out of 128 designs per group are: €0/t 0/0, €10/t 0/0, €20/t 4/3, €30/t 17/15, €40/t 34/27, and €50/t 41/41 (protein enabled / bypass). These are conditional proxy results from the current sampled design set; the positive cases must be checked against full project assumptions before any investment conclusion.
