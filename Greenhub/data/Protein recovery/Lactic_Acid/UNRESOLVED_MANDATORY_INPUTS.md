# Unresolved Mandatory Inputs

The current literature layer is sufficient to define the process concept and several endpoint checks, but it is not sufficient to run a defensible dynamic fermentation model without calibration.

Critical unresolved inputs:
- strain-specific maximum growth rate and Monod constants in actual grass juice;
- sugar-specific uptake and biomass/lactate yields;
- inoculum dose, adaptation protocol and lag time;
- pH and undissociated-lactic-acid inhibition;
- maintenance and decay;
- complete pH, sugar, lactate, by-product, biomass and soluble-protein time series;
- fermentation residence time;
- mixing, oxygen/redox conditions and heat release;
- separator capture, washing, cake moisture and purity;
- equipment-specific capacity, cost, energy demand and scaling exponent.

These variables must remain explicit required inputs or calibration targets.
