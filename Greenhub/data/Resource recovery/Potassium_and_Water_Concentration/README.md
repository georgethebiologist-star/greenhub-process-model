# GreenHub Potassium and Water Concentration dataset suite

Scope: evaporation, NF, RO, VSEP-RO, electrodialysis, selective ED, membrane distillation and hybrids for digestate liquids.

## Product naming safeguard
No concentrate is called a pure potassium product by default. A potassium-product claim requires measured K, NH4-N, Na, Ca, Mg, P, chloride, sulfate, COD, water and other relevant constituents plus an explicit purity specification.

## Route evidence
Vacuum evaporation at pH 6 produced a mixed nutrient concentrate. Pilot RO achieved >85% rejection after extensive pretreatment. Selective ED recovered >95% ammonium and potassium but produced mixed ionic streams with NaCl. Pilot ED concentrate contained both 7.1 kg NH4-N/m3 and 2.49 kg K/m3. MD fouling became concentration-factor dependent from CF 3 onward.

## Scaling
Use activity-based mineral saturation calculations at scenario temperature and ionic strength.

## Costs and energy
Unsupported costs, duties, energy use, life and scaling exponents are blank. Cost scaling is disabled without reference cost, scale, cost year and exponent.

Use parameter_id as immutable provenance and python_variable as the integration key.
