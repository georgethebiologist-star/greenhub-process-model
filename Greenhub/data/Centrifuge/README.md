# GreenHub Centrifugation Dataset Suite

Path: `Greenhub/data/Centrifuge_and_Separation/Centrifugation/`

The suite covers laboratory centrifugation, industrial decanter centrifuges, disc-stack clarification, a two-stage decanter-plus-disc-stack sequence, and a guarded hydrocyclone scenario.

## Scale guardrail
Laboratory RCF and time define a batch protocol only. They do not provide industrial feed capacity, sigma factor, solids-conveyance capacity, torque behavior, power, discharge interval, fouling response or continuous capture.

## Evidence hierarchy
Direct fresh green-juice evidence is retained separately from digestate, laboratory, fermentation-proxy and manufacturer equipment rows. Digestate performance is never silently transferred to grass juice.

## Equation limitations
Stokes and sigma equations assume dilute approximately spherical particles and Newtonian flow. Grass juice and digestate may contain fibres, flocs and non-Newtonian phases. Use equations for screening only until grade-efficiency data are measured.

## Costs
Historical SYSTEMIC costs retain EUR and 2020 basis. Used equipment retains original listing currency. No escalation, currency conversion or scaling exponent is invented.

Missing values remain blank and must not be replaced by zero.
