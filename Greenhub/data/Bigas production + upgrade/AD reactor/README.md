# GreenHub AD Reactor Engineering

Traceable reactor geometry, operating windows, mixing, heat loss, recycle, startup, cost, scenario and equation datasets. Values are source-specific. Missing values remain blank. Whole-project costs are not vessel costs; vendor mixing data are vendor-specific; small-scale gas storage factors are not industrial design rules.

`brown_juice_reactor_benchmarks.csv` stages literature results from a continuous UASB study of green-biorefinery brown juice. It deliberately keeps continuous reactor yields separate from batch BMP values. The reported 202 L CH4/kg COD and 307 L CH4/kg VS at 13.9 g COD/L/day and 3-day HRT are study-specific operating results, not GreenHub defaults. The abstract separately reports batch yields up to 500 L CH4/kg VS after 15 days; this upper result is also not a default. Feedstock and separation-boundary similarity must be checked before any calculation connection. This report-only benchmark does not activate an AD reactor equation or promote the dataset to D4.

`feng_2021_brown_juice_anaerobic_filter_abstract_reference.csv` adds two abstract-level pilot observations for grass-clover brown juice: up to 80% influent-COD removal at 5.5-day HRT and the abstract's reported specific-CH expression. The latter is preserved verbatim with unresolved dimensional interpretation and no normalized value. Neither observation is a GreenHub default or calculation input. A secondary review's VS-normalized continuous-yield attribution is not imported because it is not substantiated by the Feng abstract and conflicts with the separate Green Valleys 90-day batch-BMP record; primary full-text reconciliation is still needed.

`guanlao_2019_roadside_grass_thermophilic_ad_reference.csv` adds 118 source-located observations from a 2019 Ghent University master's dissertation. Batch 1 is dried/ensiled Belgian roadside grass; Batch 2 is separately identified managed grassland. The extract includes feed solids and source-calculated methane-potential results, two pilot CSTR configurations and operating conditions, reported specific methane productivity, separate enzyme-treatment comparisons, and the commercial bird-seed sanitation proxy. Reported treatment tables and the thesis's summary table differ slightly, so both are retained rather than reconciled. Digestate nutrient assays are listed as methods only because their numeric nutrient results are not tabulated in the accessible thesis. These records are B/reference-only and do not activate runtime AD or fertilizer calculations.

`ryegrass_silage_two_phase_maus_2020_process_reference.csv` stores peer-reviewed two-stage two-phase ryegrass-silage reactor conditions and selected gas, VFA, and ammonium observations from Maus et al. (2020), DOI `10.1186/s40793-020-00354-x`. The paired material profile in `biorefinery_platform/examples/material_profiles/perennial_ryegrass_silage_maus_2020.json` keeps feed analytical properties separate from mutually exclusive mass composition. `L_N/kg VS` is the paper's normal-volume basis (0 °C, 1013 hPa); biogas, methane, batch BMP, and stage gas-composition observations remain distinct. The article's reported ± spreads are not relabeled as standard deviations. These are literature-backed B observations for a ryegrass-silage, two-stage biofilm system—not GreenHub raw grass or the active single-stage CSTR model—and remain reference-only.
