# Phase 7 Product Definitions

## Nutrient-Rich Digestate Solids

This is the S701 cake including captured particulate organics, biomass, particulate nutrients, inerts, contaminants, and entrained centrate. Reports show wet mass, dry mass, C/N/P/K, water, and every aggregate contaminant. It is not classified as a fertilizer or soil improver.

## Struvite Product

The target formula is `MgNH4PO4.6H2O`. Product mass may include newly formed struvite, physically recovered upstream struvite, co-captured mineral solids, contaminants, and mother liquor. Reports distinguish:

- kg wet product/h;
- kg struvite/h;
- kg P/h and kg N/h in struvite;
- moisture and target-mineral dry-basis purity;
- Mg/base demand and counterions;
- competing precipitates and contaminants.

The model does not certify crystal identity, particle size, purity, agronomic availability, or regulatory eligibility.

## Calcium-Phosphate Product

The selected bookkeeping formula may be calcium phosphate, dicalcium phosphate, or hydroxyapatite. A selected formula enforces stoichiometry but is not an XRD phase claim. Product reports include Ca and P content, moisture, co-captured solids, and contaminants.

## Ammonium Product

The stripping route produces a liquid ammonium-sulfate or ammonium-chloride solution. It contains explicit salt, solution water, residual free acid, and absorbed CO2 where configured. No crystallization or dry fertilizer is claimed.

NR6 produces an ammonium-rich regeneration solution containing NH4, chloride, residual NaCl, and rinse water. It is not labelled ammonium salt fertilizer and requires further product/recycle design.

## Potassium-Rich Concentrate

The K stream is a residual-liquor concentrate, not a selectively purified potassium salt. K, Na, Cl, sulfate, residual ammonium, organics, and contaminants can all increase in concentration. Reports include K kg/h, K kg/m3, co-salts, contaminants, and water content.

## Treated-Water Candidate

Only the membrane option creates a liquid permeate candidate. It can still contain rejected-passage nutrients, salts, organics, and contaminants. Reuse or discharge requires an applicable standard and accredited analysis. Evaporator vapor is reported as off-gas/vapor rather than reusable water because condensation and polishing are not modelled.

## Analytical Bases

Product reports keep these quantities separate:

- kg N/h versus kg NH4/h or kg NH3/h;
- kg P/h versus kg PO4/h or kg struvite/h;
- kg K/h versus kg K2O-equivalent;
- wet product mass versus dry solids and target-mineral mass.

TAN, TKN, TP, COD, TOC, and nutrient recoveries are calculated metrics. They are not additional physical components.

## Finishing And Storage

Wet, washed, and low-temperature dried P products are supported structurally. Washing and drying are disabled by default. V722 and V741 report steady hold-up from residence time but no vendor tank cost. Product handling, stability, corrosion, packaging, storage loss, and logistics require project data.

## Compliance Boundary

No Phase 7 stream is certified for fertilizer use, land application, discharge, or water reuse. Required evidence includes selected Dutch/EU product pathway, complete accredited chemistry, heavy metals and organic contaminants, pathogens/sanitation, product stability, and agronomic performance.
