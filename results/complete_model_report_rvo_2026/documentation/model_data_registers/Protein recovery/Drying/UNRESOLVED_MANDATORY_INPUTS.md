# Unresolved Mandatory Inputs

A defensible GreenHub drying model still requires direct grass/leaf-protein measurements for:

## Spray drying
- feed viscosity and solids limits;
- atomizer settings and droplet Dv10/Dv50/Dv90;
- drying-air flow and humidity;
- residence-time distribution;
- evaporation and outlet-moisture time/run data;
- powder recovery, cyclone/filter loss and wall deposition;
- protein denaturation, aggregation and solubility retention;
- particle size, bulk density, morphology and flowability;
- measured heat and electrical demand.

## Freeze drying
- freezing rate, nucleation and ice-crystal data;
- shelf, chamber and product-temperature profiles;
- collapse/eutectic/glass-transition temperatures;
- cake resistance and sublimation rate;
- primary and secondary drying endpoints;
- residual-moisture versus stability response;
- total energy and cycle time.

## Equipment and economics
- capacity and equipment configuration;
- purchased cost;
- module/chamber/dryer lifetime;
- maintenance and replacement costs;
- equipment-specific scaling factors.

All unresolved values remain blank in the CSV package.
