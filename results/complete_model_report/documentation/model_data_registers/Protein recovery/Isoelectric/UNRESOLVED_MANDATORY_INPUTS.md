# Unresolved Mandatory Inputs Report

The most consequential unresolved inputs are:

1. **Roadside-grass protein pI distribution.** A heterogeneous grass-protein pool cannot be represented by one universal pI.
2. **Raw press-juice solubility versus pH and temperature.** Available ryegrass values are mainly for processed concentrates/powders.
3. **Feed-specific titration and acid/base demand.** Target pH alone cannot determine reagent consumption.
4. **Time-resolved precipitation and aggregation kinetics.** No defensible grass-specific rate constant or residence time was found.
5. **Separator capture, cake moisture and washing response.** These must be measured for the selected centrifuge/filter and slurry state.
6. **Equipment-specific purchase cost, scale exponent and power.** Obtain quotations or validated cost curves on a consistent scope/year basis.
7. **Protein stability and functionality during acidic holding.** Required when food/feed functionality is an objective.

Until these are resolved, the BioSTEAM unit should expose them as mandatory scenario inputs or calibration targets rather than hidden defaults.
