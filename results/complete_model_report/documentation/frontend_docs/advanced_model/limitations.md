# Advanced Model Limitations

## Checkpoint 1 Scope

This checkpoint is a governance and architecture release. It does not make the process
model more mechanistic, dynamic, calibrated, cost-complete, or project-validated.

## Data

- D3 indicates structural loadability only.
- All 301 literature datasets still require row-level applicability review before use.
- 278 row identities are quarantined for 443 reported issues.
- Source status labels are not yet mapped into the canonical value-status vocabulary.
- Transferability scores and uncertainty are not resolved.
- No literature observation is a GreenHub default.

## Equations And Dynamics

- No literature equation is executable through the new architecture.
- No arbitrary expression evaluation is permitted.
- 169 of 179 unique equation identities are blocked; the remaining 10 have no typed
  implementation or required scientific tests.
- No dynamic state, controller, event, solver, uncertainty model, or progress callback
  is implemented.

## Process And Utilities

- All process outputs remain the structural alpha calculations and limitations.
- Shared CHP and plant utility networks remain absent.
- Current protein, digestion, upgrading, and nutrient-recovery simplifications remain.
- Configuration comparison remains an engineering inventory and is not a defensible
  optimum or ranking.

## Economics And Products

- Twenty-four possible cost-overlap groups remain blocked.
- Missing costs, lifetimes, maintenance, and consumables remain missing.
- No TEA, LCA, MCDA, product certification, fertilizer eligibility, or water-reuse
  claim is supported.

## Application Layer

- The API-neutral service layer and serializable run directories are designs only.
- No concurrency, cancellation, dashboard override, or persistent run service exists.
- No advanced plots or machine-generated plot files exist in this checkpoint.
