# PR7 biostimulant screening figures

Benchmark PNGs visualize supplied hormone and membrane tables and are screening inputs, not full-route results. The cited 2025 alfalfa brown-juice paper reports non-fermented IAA in a different stream; it does not validate the PR7 clarified-grass-juice concentrations or free/bound fractions, and it did not assess cytokinins. Those remain user-supplied screening values. The supplied free/bound fractions are discussed in a 14-day anaerobic context and are unvalidated for the current no-fermentation PR7 boundary. The PR7 mass-distribution PNG is generated from an actual BioSTEAM SimulationService run; it shows the clarified-juice mass split and explicit unresolved outlet. The technology-comparison PNG and CSV contain separate actual full-plant TUF, LNF and MEUF runs, including unresolved outlets and the known-permeate area floor. The seasonal-operation PNG and CSV come from a 12-month quasi-dynamic PR7 run and report actual modelled monthly feed, product, unresolved, volume, and utility flows. The membrane feed boundary is clarified grass juice with no 14-day pre-membrane fermentation/storage; downstream digestion remains controlled by the separate AD scenario. MEUF hormone retention is shown using the generic midpoint specified in the supplied data. TUF free-hormone retention uses the sigma/Peclet estimate at the nominal 22 L/(m²·h) flux, while TUF bound and LNF free/bound retentions use registered state-specific screening factors. Water hold-up is derived from the target VCF=10 to form a liquid retentate; it is not a measured membrane rejection.

Regenerate with `.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/generate_biostimulant_figures.py`. Each PNG is exported at 300 DPI; figure dimensions are approximately 2400 px wide. The SDS price-context figure shows public catalog references only; they are excluded from process TEA. The capital-reference chart separates contextual grass-biorefinery investments from the PR7 membrane-media price basis; none of the analogs are used in modeled CAPEX. The membrane-GWP reference chart separates kg CO₂-eq/m² hollow-fiber fabrication values from kg CO₂-eq/module spiral-wound analogs; both are excluded from modeled LCA totals because materials, geometry and inventories do not match PR7. The local and report-wide figure indexes are updated reproducibly.
